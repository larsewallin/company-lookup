Company Lookup

Find a company anywhere in the world and see who it is, whether it is active, who owns it, and whether anything looks wrong. Free checks from public registers, with every fact linked to its source.

For information only. Not advice, a credit rating or a credit report, and not for decisions about individuals. Flags are prompts to check, not findings. Confirm anything important at the source.

Coverage

Sources so far: GLEIF (global legal entity register); national registers for the UK, France, Norway, Finland, Estonia, Czechia, Slovakia, Latvia, Poland (KRS number), Switzerland, Ireland, Israel, Singapore, Hong Kong (live companies), Taiwan, Australia, Colombia, Canada (federal corporations and Quebec), Argentina (national companies register), Chile (companies formed since 2013), Peru (SUNAT tax register), Ukraine (legal entities), India (companies and LLPs registered since 2016), the Dominican Republic (DGII taxpayer register), Ecuador (SRI RUC register), Paraguay (DNIT RUC register), Russia (tax service open data: organisations that filed accounts, with revenue), Moldova (State Register), New Caledonia (RIDET), Romania (ONRC trade register), Luxembourg (companies that filed annual accounts, with key figures), Slovenia, Cyprus (monthly open data) and Brazil (CNPJ number); US: SEC EDGAR plus the New York, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Oregon and Pennsylvania state registers; the EU VAT check (VIES); Wikidata (CC0); RDAP domain records. Pick a country to search its national register. More national registers are being added one at a time. Flags come from fixed written rules and are prompts to check, not findings of fraud. This is not a credit rating and is not for decisions about individuals.
